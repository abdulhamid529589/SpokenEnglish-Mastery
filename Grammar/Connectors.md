## 1. Class Overview

- **Class:** 10th of the spoken English series (the course was near its end; one or two classes plus a pronunciation class remained).
- **Promise of the class:** a new technique for speaking English *without translating from Hindi*.
- **Method:** warm up with drills → learn the technique → practise scenarios → learn 4 new speaking patterns.
- **Teacher's key message:** *Feel* the sentence. Do not memorise translations. Translation-based learning is the slowest way to speak.

---

## 2. The Five Building Blocks

The technique only works if these five are already on your tongue (they were practised in earlier classes):

| # | Building block | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **is / am / are / was / were / has / have** | State and possession sentences |
| 2 | **Tenses** — only *Present Indefinite* and *Past Indefinite* (plus continuous forms) | Habits, facts, past events, ongoing actions |
| 3 | **Voice** — passive with the **third form** of the verb | "It is spoken", "I was taught" |
| 4 | **Modals** — can, could, may, might, should, would, must, have to | Ability, permission, advice, politeness |
| 5 | **Phrases/connectors** — as far as I am concerned, I would like to say, I think, I believe, and, but, after that, then, again | Starting and linking your speech |

Bonus pattern: **to + verb** (*I want to play, he wants to go*).

## 3. Warm-up Drills: Making the 4 Types of Sentences

For every word or verb you can think of, make **four sentences**: positive, negative, question, and wh-question.

### 3.1 With is/am/are/has/have

| Type | Example | বাংলা |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | He is honest. | সে সৎ। |
| Negative | He is not honest. | সে সৎ নয়। |
| Question | Is he honest? | সে কি সৎ? |
| Wh-question | Why is he honest? / How is he honest? | সে কেন/কীভাবে সৎ? |
| Positive (has) | He has time. | তার সময় আছে। |
| Negative | He doesn't have time. | তার সময় নেই। |
| Question | Does he have time? | তার কি সময় আছে? |
| Positive (have) | I have his number. | আমার কাছে তার নম্বর আছে। |
| Negative | I don't have her number. | আমার কাছে তার নম্বর নেই। |
| Question | Do you have my number? | তোমার কাছে কি আমার নম্বর আছে? |

Practice words used: *good, happy, sad, honest, selfish, teacher, stranger, time, love, peace, mobile phone, number.*

### 3.2 Present Indefinite (habits/facts)

| Type | Example | বাংলা |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | I play cricket. | আমি ক্রিকেট খেলি। |
| Negative | I don't play cricket. | আমি ক্রিকেট খেলি না। |
| Question | Do you play cricket? | তুমি কি ক্রিকেট খেলো? |
| Wh-question | When / where do you play cricket? | তুমি কখন/কোথায় ক্রিকেট খেলো? |
| Positive | He understands me. | সে আমাকে বোঝে। |
| Negative | He doesn't understand me. | সে আমাকে বোঝে না। |
| Question | Does my father understand me? | আমার বাবা কি আমাকে বোঝেন? |
| Wh-question | What do you teach? | তুমি কী পড়াও? |

Practice verbs: *speak, go, come, say, tell, write, see, like, love, understand, read, know, teach, give, take, laugh.*

### 3.3 Present Continuous (happening now)

| Type | Example | বাংলা |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | I am watching a movie. | আমি একটি সিনেমা দেখছি। |
| Negative | I am not playing football. | আমি ফুটবল খেলছি না। |
| Question | Is he sending a message? | সে কি মেসেজ পাঠাচ্ছে? |
| Wh-question | Why are you playing with my feelings? | তুমি কেন আমার অনুভূতি নিয়ে খেলছ? |
| Positive | He is beating me. | সে আমাকে মারছে। |
| Negative | He is not giving me time. | সে আমাকে সময় দিচ্ছে না। |

**Teacher's tip:** use real-life sentences, not only "I play cricket". Try *He is playing with my feelings*, *She is not giving me importance*, *He is not laughing in the class*.

### 3.4 Past Indefinite (second form)

| Type | Example | বাংলা |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | I saw you there. | আমি তোমাকে সেখানে দেখেছিলাম। |
| Negative | I didn't see you in the class. | আমি তোমাকে ক্লাসে দেখিনি। |
| Question | Did I see you in the class? | আমি কি তোমাকে ক্লাসে দেখেছিলাম? |
| Positive | I wrote a letter to the Prime Minister. | আমি প্রধানমন্ত্রীকে একটি চিঠি লিখেছিলাম। |
| Negative | I didn't write a letter to the Prime Minister. | আমি লিখিনি। |
| Question | Did India win the World Cup? | ভারত কি বিশ্বকাপ জিতেছিল? |
| Positive | My father loved me. | আমার বাবা আমাকে ভালোবাসতেন। |
| Wh-question | Why didn't your father love you? | তোমার বাবা কেন তোমাকে ভালোবাসেননি? |
| Extended | I learned English from YouTube. / Did you learn English from Dear Sir? | আমি ইউটিউব থেকে ইংরেজি শিখেছি। |

### 3.5 Past Continuous (was/were + verb-ing)

| Type | Example | বাংলা |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | I was reading a book. | আমি একটি বই পড়ছিলাম। |
| Negative | I wasn't doing anything. | আমি কিছুই করছিলাম না। |
| Question | Were you writing something? | তুমি কি কিছু লিখছিলে? |
| Positive | I was telling a story. | আমি একটি গল্প বলছিলাম। |
| Negative | I wasn't going to my home. | আমি বাড়ি যাচ্ছিলাম না। |
| Question | Was I going to my home? | আমি কি বাড়ি যাচ্ছিলাম? |

## 4. Feel the Tenses: Do/Does vs Was/Were

The teacher used a **visualisation exercise** so you *feel* the tense instead of translating:

| Word | Imagine | Feeling |
|---|---|---|
| **do / does** | Big **white** letters glowing in the sky, like the moon | **Present** — now, alive, current, habit |
| **was / were** | Big **dark** letters in the dark, faint | **Past** — over, gone, yesterday |

**Use it:** the moment you see or think *do/does* → your mind should say "today, habit, present". The moment you see *was/were* → "old, finished, yesterday". Then make sentences with that feeling instead of translating Hindi.

**Example sentences**
- *I read the news every morning.* (present habit — white)
- *I was reading the news when you called.* (past ongoing — dark)

## 5. Passive Voice in Speaking

**Rule of thumb:** in Hindi, passive sounds like **"…जाता है / …गया"** (is done / was done). Use *be-verb + third form*.

| Type | Example | বাংলা |
|---|---|---|
| Present | English is spoken here. | এখানে ইংরেজি বলা হয়। |
| Negative | English is not spoken here. | এখানে ইংরেজি বলা হয় না। |
| Question | Is English spoken here? | এখানে কি ইংরেজি বলা হয়? |
| Wh-question | Where is English spoken? / How is English spoken? | কোথায়/কীভাবে ইংরেজি বলা হয়? |
| Past | He was taken to the hospital. | তাকে হাসপাতালে নিয়ে যাওয়া হয়েছিল। |
| Negative | He was not taken to the hospital. | তাকে নেওয়া হয়নি। |
| Past | I was taught English. | আমাকে ইংরেজি শেখানো হয়েছিল। |
| Past | The ball was caught. | বলটি ধরা হয়েছিল। |
| Past | I was not given time. | আমাকে সময় দেওয়া হয়নি। |
| Past | She was not given love. | তাকে ভালোবাসা দেওয়া হয়নি। |
| With agent | The World Cup was won by India. | বিশ্বকাপ ভারতের দ্বারা জেতা হয়েছিল। |
| With agent | English is spoken by me. | ইংরেজি আমার দ্বারা বলা হয়। |
| Longer | I was taught by Dear Sir. / She was loved by her mother. | আমাকে স্যার পড়িয়েছিলেন। |

**Important:** always keep a **be-verb** (is/am/are/was/were). "She loved by her mother" ❌ → "She was loved by her mother" ✅. Only the **third form** of the verb is used.

## 6. Modals in Speaking

| Modal | Use | বাংলা | Example Sentence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **can** | Ability, permission | পারা / পারি | I can speak English. |
| **can't / cannot** | Inability | পারি না | I cannot forget you. |
| **could** | Softer request or past ability | পারতাম / দয়া করে | Could you please pass me the bottle? |
| **may** | Polite permission, possibility | অনুমতি চাওয়া / হতে পারে | May I come in, sir? / It may rain today. |
| **might** | Weak possibility | হয়তো | He might not come. |
| **should** | Advice | উচিত | Should I go? / You should tell me. |
| **would** | Polite offer / request / imagined situation | ...করবেন কি? / ...করতাম | Would you like some tea? |
| **must** | Strong obligation | অবশ্যই করতে হবে | He must be busy. / You must be at the hospital. |
| **has to / have to** | Compulsion | করতেই হবে | I have to go. / I don't have to work. |
| **will / shall** | Future | করব | I will call you. |
| **could have / should have** | Past regret or missed chance | করতে পারতাম / করা উচিত ছিল | You should have told me. |

**Note (class question):** for *may* questions, start with *may*: *May I not sit here?* — do not begin with "Don't". For the future, use **will** (not *would*) in general speech.

## 7. The Main Technique: Pick Your Helping Verb in Half a Second

### The insight
Every fluent (non-native) speaker does this in the mind: **before saying a question, they decide *what type* of question it is** and instantly pick the **first word** (a helping verb/modal). The rest of the sentence follows naturally.

### The three-step method
1. **Thought** — what do I want to say? (Picture the thing: *momos*, *iPhone*, *friend*.)
2. **Type** — is it about ability, offer, habit, past, possession, state, permission or advice?
3. **First word** — pick the matching helping verb, then add the subject and the rest.

### The helper-verb chooser

| What you want to ask/say | First word | Example (English) | বাংলা |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ability / can you do it?** | **Can** | Can you drive a car? | তুমি কি গাড়ি চালাতে পারো? |
| **Polite request** | **Could / Would** | Could you please give me the water bottle? | আপনি কি দয়া করে জলের বোতলটা দেবেন? |
| **Offer** | **Would** | Would you like some tea? | আপনি কি চা খাবেন? |
| **Permission** | **May / Can** | May I use your phone? | আমি কি আপনার ফোনটা ব্যবহার করতে পারি? |
| **Advice** | **Should** | Should I go now? | আমার কি এখন যাওয়া উচিত? |
| **Habit / routine / likes** | **Do / Does** | Do you like momos? / Do you use Instagram? | তুমি কি মোমো পছন্দ করো? |
| **Past event** | **Did** | Did you say anything? | তুমি কি কিছু বলেছিলে? |
| **Possession ("have")** | **Do … have / Have** | Do you have an iPhone? | তোমার কি আইফোন আছে? |
| **Past experience** | **Have** | Have you seen the Taj Mahal? / Have you had food? | তুমি কি তাজমহল দেখেছ? |
| **State / feeling / identity** | **Is / Am / Are** | Are you fine? / Is she your sister? | তুমি কি ঠিক আছো? |
| **State in the past** | **Was / Were** | Was the class over? | ক্লাস কি শেষ হয়ে গিয়েছিল? |

### How to spot the type quickly
- Question about **"is it there / does he own it"** → *have*.
- Question about **habit/likes/regular action** → *do / does*.
- Question about **something that happened** → *did*.
- Question about **"what is it like / how is he"** → *is / are*.
- Question asking **someone to do something for you** → *could / would*.
- Question about **skill or power** → *can*.

### Wh-words
For "why, when, where, how, what" simply put the wh-word **before** the helping verb:
- *When do you eat momos?* / *Why did you do that?* / *How did you win the World Cup?* / *Where is English spoken?*

## 8. Scenario Practice (Question Building)

The teacher gave many situations; here they are with the model answers.

| Situation | Model question | Helper chosen | বাংলা |

| Invite someone for tea | Would you like some tea? | would | আপনি কি চা খাবেন? |
| Ask a stranger's name | May I know your name? / What is your name? | may / is | আপনার নামটা জানতে পারি? |
| Ask a friend if you should leave | Should I go now? | should | আমি কি এখন যাব? |
| Ask if your parents understand you | Do my parents understand me? / Do they understand me? | do | আমার বাবা-মা কি আমাকে বোঝেন? |
| Ask if a friend likes peace | Do you like peace? | do | তুমি কি শান্তি পছন্দ করো? |

| Ask if you've met someone before | Have I met you before? | have | আমি কি আগে তোমার সঙ্গে দেখা করেছি? |
| Ask if a friend said something | Did you say something? | did | তুমি কি কিছু বললে? |
| Ask if a friend has an iPhone | Do you have an iPhone? | do…have | তোমার কি আইফোন আছে? |
| Ask if a friend has time to talk | Do you have time to talk? | do…have | তোমার কি কথা বলার সময় আছে? |
| Ask if a friend's health is fine | Are you fine? / Are you feeling better? | are | তুমি কি ঠিক আছো? |
| Ask if the class is over (entering late) | Is the class over? / Did the class end? | is / did | ক্লাস কি শেষ? |
| Ask if a friend has eaten at a wedding | Have you had the food? | have | তুমি কি খেয়েছ? |
| Ask if that is his little sister | Is she your sister? | is | ও কি তোমার বোন? |
| Ask someone for the water bottle | Could you please pass me the bottle? | could | দয়া করে বোতলটা দেবেন? |
| Ask a cricketer how he won | How did you win the World Cup? | did (past) | তুমি কীভাবে বিশ্বকাপ জিতলে? |
| Ask if he eats momos | Do you eat momos? | do | তুমি কি মোমো খাও? |
| Ask how something is | How are the momos? (hot/cold?) | are | মোমোগুলো কেমন? |

**Answering style (same logic):**
- Own something → **have**: *I have a phone.*
- Habit → **do**: *I don't watch movies.* / *I don't like momos.*
- Ability → **can**: *I can't forget you.*

## 9. Why the "2 Seconds" Becomes Half a Second (Muscle Memory)

- At first you may need **2 seconds** to pick the helping verb. This is normal.
- With practice, the same pause becomes **half a second**, and later almost nothing.
- **Reason (muscle memory):** just as a batsman's bat moves before he thinks after thousands of balls, your mouth will reach the correct first word automatically after thousands of sentences.
- The teacher's own fluency is the same: *"When I start speaking" is in my mouth — I don't need to think.*
- **Warning:** if you keep translating from Hindi (*ता है, ती है, चुका था…*), your brain will always be slower and you will get stuck. Learn to *feel* the sentence.
- **Fluent speakers who seem to "pause"** (film stars, sportspeople in old interviews) are not failing — they are taking half a second to choose the question type.

## 10. New Spoken Connectors

### 10.1 As well as (…also / …too)

**Rule:** put **"as well as" at the end** of the sentence to say "also".

| Example | বাংলা |
|---|---|
| I am happy as well as proud. | আমি খুশি এবং গর্বিতও। |
| He is a doctor as well as a teacher. | সে ডাক্তার এবং শিক্ষকও। |
| She is tired as well. | সেও ক্লান্ত। |
| I am a lawyer and a teacher as well. | আমি একজন আইনজীবী এবং শিক্ষকও। |
| I have a house and a car as well. | আমার একটি বাড়ি এবং একটি গাড়িও আছে। |
| Dear Sir is handsome as well. | ডিয়ার স্যারও সুদর্শন। |

> Sounds more impressive than "also".

### 10.2 Not only … but also

| Pattern | Example | বাংলা |
|---|---|---|
| Not only + noun/adjective … but also … | He is not only a good teacher but also a good human being. | সে শুধু ভালো শিক্ষকই নয়, ভালো মানুষও। |
| | He is not only my friend but also a good person. | সে শুধু আমার বন্ধুই নয়, ভালো মানুষও। |
| | Shraddha Kapoor is not only an actress but also a good singer. | শ্রদ্ধা কাপুর শুধু অভিনেত্রীই নয়, ভালো গায়িকাও। |
| | He is not only handsome but also intelligent. | সে শুধু সুদর্শনই নয়, বুদ্ধিমানও। |
| | I am not only a teacher but also a student. | আমি শুধু শিক্ষকই নয়, শিক্ষার্থীও। |

> Use it to say someone has **two qualities**.

### 10.3 Keep / Kept + verb-ing (keep doing something)

| Tense | Example | বাংলা |
|---|---|---|
| Present | I keep trying. | আমি চেষ্টা করেই যাই। |
| Present | She keeps thinking. / She keeps smiling. | সে ভাবতেই থাকে। / হাসতেই থাকে। |
| Present | They keep watching movies. | তারা সিনেমা দেখতেই থাকে। |
| Present | I keep thinking about him. | আমি তার কথা ভাবতেই থাকি। |
| Present | I keep on practising English. | আমি ইংরেজি অনুশীলন করতেই থাকি। |
| Present | She keeps on dancing. | সে নাচতেই থাকে। |
| Past | I kept trying. | আমি চেষ্টা করেই গেলাম। |
| Past | I kept thinking about him. | আমি তার কথা ভাবতেই থাকলাম। |
| Past | They kept watching movies. | তারা সিনেমা দেখতেই থাকল। |
| With "on" | He keeps on working hard. | সে কঠোর পরিশ্রম করেই যায়। |

**Also:** *keep* as a plain verb means "to hold/possess": *I keep a wallet with me.* / *I keep good people around me.*

### 10.4 Get / Got / Getting (the most useful verb in speaking)

**Meaning 1 — obtain / receive / understand**

| Example | বাংলা |
|---|---|
| I got success. / I didn't get success. | আমি সফলতা পেয়েছি। / পাইনি। |
| I got a friend. | আমি একজন বন্ধু পেয়েছি। |
| I didn't get your letter. | আমি তোমার চিঠি পাইনি। |
| I could not get my pen / shoes. | আমি আমার কলম / জুতো খুঁজে পাইনি। |
| I got a ₹10 note. | আমি ১০ টাকার নোট পেয়েছি। |
| Did you get me? | তুমি কি আমাকে বুঝলে? |
| I didn't get you. / I'm not getting you. | আমি তোমাকে বুঝতে পারিনি। / পারছি না। |
| I have not got him. | আমি তাকে বুঝিনি। |
| I want to get an ice cream. / Get me this. | আমি একটা আইসক্রিম চাই। / আমাকে এটা এনে দাও। |

> When the person's **words or point** are not understood, English uses **get** ("I didn't get you" = I didn't understand).

**Meaning 2 — become (change of state); the "जाना / हो जाना" meaning**

| Example | বাংলা |
|---|---|
| I get tired. | আমি ক্লান্ত হয়ে যাই। |
| She gets tired. | সে ক্লান্ত হয়ে যায়। |
| I am getting tired. | আমি ক্লান্ত হয়ে পড়ছি। |
| I got tired. | আমি ক্লান্ত হয়ে গিয়েছিলাম। |
| I get confused. | আমি কনফিউজড হয়ে যাই। |
| She gets confused. | সে কনফিউজড হয়ে যায়। |
| He gets sick / ill. | সে অসুস্থ হয়ে পড়ে। |
| I am getting confused. | আমি বিভ্রান্ত হয়ে পড়ছি। |
| He got scared. | সে ভয় পেয়ে গেল। |
| I got hurt. | আমি আঘাত পেলাম। |

**How to decide:** if you are talking about something *changing* ("I become tired / he gets scared"), use **get + adjective**. The word after *get* is normally an **adjective** (tired, sick, scared, confused, hurt). A verb's **third form** can act as an adjective here (*tired, confused, scared, done*).

**Don't do this:**
- ❌ *She gets play* → get + verb is wrong; use an adjective: *She gets tired.*
- ❌ *I get confuse* → ✅ *I get confused.*
- The habit sentence uses the base verb with **-s**: *She gets sick*. Past: *She got sick*. Continuous: *I am getting confused* (not "I am getting confuse").

### 10.5 Into (a passion or hobby)

| Example | বাংলা |
|---|---|
| I am into cricket. | আমি ক্রিকেট ভালোবাসি / আমার ক্রিকেটের নেশা আছে। |
| I am into cooking. | আমার রান্নার শখ আছে। |
| I am not into politics. | আমি রাজনীতিতে আগ্রহী নই। |
| I am not into music / games / dancing / running. | আমি গান / খেলা / নাচ / দৌড়ে আগ্রহী নই। |
| I am into singing. | আমার গানের শখ আছে। |

> Use **into** only for hobbies/interests — not for places. ("I am not into Mumbai" is wrong.)

## 11. Doubts Answered in Class

| Doubt | Answer |
|---|---|
| In **future tense**, should I use *would*? | Mostly use **will**. *Would* is for polite requests or imagined situations. |
| Does *may* need "don't" in questions? | No. Say **May I…?** / **May I not…?** |
| "Is he not well?" vs "Isn't he well?" | Very small difference; use either for now. |
| I translate in my head before speaking. | Use today's technique: pick the helper by *type*, not by Hindi. |
| I mix up *do* and *did*. | *Do* = present habit (white/now); *did* = past event (dark/finished). |
| Writing takes time to think. | The more you think and write/speak, the faster it becomes. |
| The phrase "I was like…" is hard. | Leave it for later; practise what already comes to your mouth first. |
| Passive/active voice practice? | Watch the separate 2-hour voice video; the class covered passive in speaking. |

## 13. Quick Revision Sheet

**Technique in 3 lines**
1. Picture the thing (momos, iPhone, friend).
2. Decide the question type (ability, offer, habit, past, possession, state).
3. Pick the first word (can / would / do / did / have / is) and add the rest.

| Pattern | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Ability | Can + you + verb? | Can you swim? |
| Polite request | Could/Would + you + please + verb? | Could you help me? |
| Habit | Do/Does + subject + verb? | Do you eat momos? |
| Past event | Did + subject + verb? | Did you call me? |
| Possession | Do/Does + subject + have? | Do you have time? |
| State | Is/Are + subject + adjective? | Are you fine? |
| Passive | be-verb + third form | English is spoken here. |
| Also | … as well | He is tired as well. |
| Two qualities | not only … but also … | He is not only kind but also smart. |
| Keep doing | keep/kept + verb-ing | I keep thinking. |
| Become | get + adjective | I get tired. |
| Interest | be + into + noun/-ing | I am into music. |
