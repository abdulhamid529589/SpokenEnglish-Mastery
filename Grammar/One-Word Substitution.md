## 3. Phobias — Fear Words

> **Key Rule:** Any word ending in **-phobia** refers to a **fear** of something specific.

| Phobia Word | Fear of | Notes |
|-------------|---------|-------|
| **Ecophobia** | One's home surroundings | Eco = home/environment |
| **Claustrophobia** | Closed or confined spaces | Very common — fear of small rooms, lifts, tunnels |
| **Hydrophobia** | Water | Hydro = water |
| **Bibliophobia** | Books | Biblio = books |
| **Xenophobia** | Foreigners or strangers | Xeno = foreign/strange |
| **Necrophobia** | Dead bodies | Necro = death |
| **Aerophobia** | Heights or aircraft | Aero = air/height |
| **Ophidiophobia / Ophiophobia** | Snakes | Also written as Ophidiphobia |
| **Syngenesophobia** | Relatives | Syngenes = related by birth |
| **Nomophobia** | Being without a mobile phone | Nomo = no mobile |
| **Zoophobia** | Animals | Zoo = animal |
| **Agoraphobia** | Open or public spaces | Agora = open marketplace |
| **Acrophobia** | Heights | Acro = peak/top |
| **Technophobia** | Technology | Techno = technology |

---

| Root in phobia word | What it tells you |

| **Biblio** | Books → Bibliophobia = fear of books |
| **Xeno** | Foreign/stranger → Xenophobia = fear of foreigners |

| **Aero** | Air/height → Aerophobia = fear of heights or aircraft |

## 4. Killings — "-cide" Words

> **Key Rule:** Any word ending in **-cide** refers to the **killing or murder** of a specific person or group.

| Word | Meaning | Memory Tip |
|------|---------|-----------|
| **Homicide** | Killing of a human being | Homo = human |
| **Suicide** | Killing of oneself | Sui = self |
| **Patricide** | Killing of one's own father | Patri = father (like "paternal") |
| **Matricide** | Killing of one's own mother | Matri = mother (like "maternal") |
| **Fratricide** | Killing of one's own brother | Fratri = brother (like "fraternity") |
| **Sororicide** | Killing of one's own sister | Soror = sister (like "sorority") |
| **Uxoricide** | Killing of one's own wife | Uxor = wife |
| **Mariticide** | Killing of one's own husband | Mariti = husband |
| **Regicide** | Killing of a king | Regi = king/royal (like "regal") |
| **Infanticide** | Killing of an infant (baby under 12 months) | Infant = baby |
| **Genocide** | Killing of an entire race or ethnic group | Geno = race/origin |
| **Parricide** | Killing of one's parents | Parri = parent |
| **Feticide** | Killing of a fetus (unborn baby) | Fetus = unborn baby |

## 7. Governments & Ruling Systems — "-cracy" Words

> **Key Rule:** **-cracy** = a system of government or rule.

| Word | Meaning | Who Rules |
|------|---------|----------|
| **Democracy** | Government by elected representatives | The people (through elections) |
| **Autocracy** | Government by one person with absolute, unlimited power | A single ruler — a dictator |
| **Bureaucracy** | Government by administrative officials and civil servants | Officials and administrators |
| **Meritocracy** | System where the most intelligent and capable people lead | Those with ability and merit |
| **Plutocracy** | Government by the wealthy class | Rich and powerful people |
| **Aristocracy** | Government by the noble or elite class | The nobility and upper class |
| **Theocracy** | Government based on divine law and religious principles | Religious leaders / God's law |
| **Monarchy** | Government by a single person of royal birth (a king or queen) | A royal person — king or queen |
| **Diarchy** | Government shared by two agencies or rulers | Two agencies jointly |
| **Oligarchy** | Government by a small group of powerful people | A small elite group |
| **Stratocracy** | Government by the military class | The military |
| **Anarchy** | Complete absence of government; total disorder and chaos | No one — complete chaos |
| **Confederacy** | A union of states or parties working together | Allied states |

## 9. Marriage — "-gamy" Words

> **Key Rule:** **-gamy** = marriage. **Miso-** = hate/dislike.

| Word | Meaning | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| **Matrimony** | The state or institution of marriage | Matri = mother/woman |
| **Monogamy** | The practice of being married to only one person at a time | Mono = one |
| **Bigamy** | The crime of marrying someone while already legally married to another person | Bi = two |
| **Polygamy** | The practice of having more than one spouse at the same time | Poly = many |
| **Polyandry** | The practice of a woman having more than one husband at the same time | Poly + Andro = many men |
| **Misogamist** | A person who hates or strongly dislikes the institution of marriage | Miso + Gamy = hate + marriage |
| **Autogamy** | Self-fertilisation — particularly in plants | Auto = self |
| **Endogamy** | The custom of marrying only within one's own tribe, caste, or community | Endo = within |
| **Exogamy** | The custom of marrying outside one's own tribe, caste, or community | Exo = outside |
| **Sologamy** | The act of marrying oneself — a symbolic commitment to oneself | Solo = alone/self |

## 10. Love & Hate — "-phile" & "miso-" Words

| **Androphile** | A person who has a strong love or attraction towards men | Andro (man) + phile (love) |
