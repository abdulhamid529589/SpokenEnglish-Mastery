## Study Tips for This Master List

1. **Don't try to memorize all 2,500+ words in one sitting.** Pick one letter-section per day (or per study session) and work through it fully before moving to the next.
2. **Cover the meaning columns** and try to recall the Bengali meaning, English meaning, and a sentence from the word alone — this is more effective than passive reading.
3. **Group revision:** many of these words cluster into families (e.g., words for "stubborn," "talkative," "poverty," "danger") — after finishing a letter-section, try listing every word you remember that fits a theme.
4. **Use the example sentences actively** — try writing one new sentence of your own for each word, using the given sentence only as a model.
5. **Revisit** — spaced repetition (reviewing a section again after 2 days, then 5 days, then 10 days) is far more effective than reading it once.
