## 1. How to Use This Guide

Vocabulary sticks best when related words are learned **together as a group**, not as an isolated list. This guide follows the class's method: words are clustered by theme (e.g., all "hate" words together, all "laziness" words together), so that recalling one member of the group helps you recall the rest.

**How to study each block:**
1. Read the block once, noting the "anchor" word (the most common/easiest one).
2. Notice how the other words in the block relate to or contrast with it.
3. Attempt the worked practice questions at the end of the block without looking back.
4. Revisit blocks you struggled with the next day — spaced repetition is what converts these from "recognized" to "recalled."

## 29. Quick Self-Test

Test yourself — cover the answers and try each one:

1. What is the synonym for "ostentatious"?
   <details><summary>Answer</summary>Showy</details>

2. What is the difference between "attack" and "retaliate"?
   <details><summary>Answer</summary>Attack is the original hostile action; retaliate is the response/counter-action to an attack already received.</details>

3. Name three synonyms for "talkative."
   <details><summary>Answer</summary>Loquacious, chatterbox, talkative (extrovert is related but not identical)</details>

4. What is the antonym of "scatter"?
   <details><summary>Answer</summary>Accumulate (or gather/amass)</details>

5. What does "equivocal" mean, and how can you remember it?
   <details><summary>Answer</summary>Ambiguous/unclear — break it into "equi" (equal) + "vocal" (voice): a statement that sounds equally like more than one meaning.</details>

6. What is the key difference between "abandon" and "abundant"?
   <details><summary>Answer</summary>Abandon = to give up/leave behind; Abundant = existing in large quantities. They only look similar — unrelated meanings.</details>

7. Name four synonyms for "lazy."
   <details><summary>Answer</summary>Sluggish, lethargic, indolent, listless (or slothful)</details>

8. What is the synonym for "inevitable"?
   <details><summary>Answer</summary>Unavoidable</details>

9. What does "sanguinity" mean?
   <details><summary>Answer</summary>Optimism/positivity, especially in a difficult situation</details>

10. What is the odd one out: Extol, Hail, Admire, Despise?
    <details><summary>Answer</summary>Despise — it means contempt/dislike, while the other three mean praise/admiration.</details>
